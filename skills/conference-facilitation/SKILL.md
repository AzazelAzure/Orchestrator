# Conference Facilitation

## Procedure

1. Name seats, providers, and decision owner; facilitation is not authority.
2. Collect attributed positions; record disagreements explicitly.
3. Produce a decision record with evidence pointers and open questions.
4. Do not close gates by consensus alone; HitM/owner disposition remains required.
