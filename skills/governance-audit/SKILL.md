# Governance Audit

## Procedure

1. Inspect grants, assignments, pins, and gate actions for the scope.
2. Confirm no upward delegation, silent waiver, or authority broadening.
3. Confirm founder step-up fields when exceptions/retries after unknown occur.
4. Confirm MCP/schedule surfaces cannot issue founder ops.
5. Emit findings for material defects; keep evidence redacted.
